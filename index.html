About Us

Know About Our Dairy Farm & Our History

Abaynesh Dairy Farm has been proudly serving the Kaffa Zone and surrounding communities of Southwest Ethiopia with premium, farm-fresh dairy products. Founded on the values of quality, sustainability, and community, our farm raises healthy cattle in a clean, natural environment to bring you the very best milk, yoghurt, and butter every day.

Our Services

Services That We Offer For Entrepreneurs

Crossbreeding

We specialize in cattle crossbreeding to enhance milk yield, improve genetics, and develop high-producing dairy breeds well-adapted to local conditions.

Feed Preprocessing

We process and formulate nutrient-rich, balanced organic feed mixtures that promote optimal cow health, high digestibility, and maximum milk output.

Care & Milking

Our experienced farm team provides daily herd care, veterinary health monitoring, and hygienic milking processes to ensure pure, fresh dairy products.
